# FoodShare Food Bank Management System (FSFB-2026)

Project planning and control documents for a **Food Bank Management System** that replaces the spreadsheets and paper logs used across FoodShare's three hubs in **Scarborough, Mississauga and North Bay**.

Group project for the **Business Information Technology** program at **Seneca Polytechnic** (2026).
**My role:** Project Manager, leading a team of seven. I was responsible for overall coordination, the schedule and sponsor liaison.

---

## The problem

FoodShare collects surplus food from grocery stores, restaurants and farms and distributes it to registered families. Its records are manual, which leads to missed entries, inventory counts that drift from what's on the shelves, and food expiring in storage before it's distributed.

## The planned system

One central system covering:

- Donor and family registration
- Food donation records (partner pickup and warehouse drop-off)
- Inventory across three warehouses, including refrigerated storage
- Transportation for two delivery trucks, with pickup and distribution scheduling
- **FIFO distribution**, so food closest to expiry goes out first
- Reports on inventory and donations
